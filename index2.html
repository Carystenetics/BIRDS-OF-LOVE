The Open Door

Every cage has a door. Most of them, if you look closely, were never really locked.

tap the cage to open it

The door was always open.

scroll down

I could have built you a cage —

gold bars, a soft perch,
every reason to stay
disguised as safety.

Instead, I left the door unlocked.

Terrified, every single day,
that you'd notice, and go.

You noticed on the first day.

You've stayed every day since.

That's not captivity.

That's not even loyalty.

That's just… choosing me,
with every door left open.

The cage was never the point. The staying was.
